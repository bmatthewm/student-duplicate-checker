MATCH SETTINGS

Short names use stricter limits. Close matches require similar names and matching school and grade (or a matching student ID). Different known districts are excluded by default. Missing district values do not exclude a pair. School, grade, district, ID and optional identifiers support matches; instrument/voice and ensemble are shown for comparison but do not count as identity conflicts; conflicting identifiers are flagged, not silently ignored. Results are candidates, not confirmed identities.
